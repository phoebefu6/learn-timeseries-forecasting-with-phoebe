An honest note on forecasting: a forecast is a probability, not a promise. This course teaches the published canon - the FPP3 textbook (Hyndman & Athanasopoulos), the Box-Jenkins ARIMA method, and Prophet (Taylor & Letham 2018) - and always carries a prediction interval, never a bare point. Deep models (DeepAR, N-BEATS, TFT) are surveyed and cited, not trained from scratch. The live forecaster is real math on a small teaching series, not a production engine - and its lesson is the field's oldest: beat the simple baseline or do not ship.
